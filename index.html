Quality of Earnings

The seller's EBITDA is a claim. A QofE turns it into evidence.

Sellers present adjusted earnings in the best light they can. That is their job. Ours is to rebuild the number from source documents, month by month over three years plus the trailing twelve months, so the price, the loan and the purchase agreement rest on earnings that are real, repeatable and still there after the owner leaves.

Scope of every QofE

Seven procedures, one adjusted number.

A

Three years plus TTM, monthly

The analysis runs month by month, not on annual summaries, so seasonality, pricing changes and the last twelve months can be read on their own.

B

Proof of cash

Reported revenue reconciled to bank deposits and merchant statements. Months that do not tie are reduced to what the deposits support, with the gap explained.

C

Add-back schedule

What was claimed, what was accepted and why. Each adjustment is tested against a source document, a non-recurrence narrative and a benchmark.

D

Book-to-tax and IRS transcripts

The P&L reconciled to the filed returns and to IRS account transcripts, obtained under one authorization and shared with the lender.

E

Customer concentration and revenue durability

Revenue by customer by year, contract terms, pricing and churn, so the reader knows whether last year's revenue is likely to repeat.

F

DSC-ready adjusted earnings

Stated as adjusted EBITDA and as cash flow available for debt service, by historical year, so the number drops straight into the lender's coverage calculation.

G

Net working capital trend

A trailing analysis of receivables, inventory and payables, and a proposed working capital peg for the purchase agreement.

WHAT IS IN THE REPORT

Signed, sourced and readable by someone who was not in the room.

Prepared under NACVA professional standards and signed by a Certified Valuation Analyst. Typical turnaround for non-lender engagements is 5 to 15 business days from a complete document set.

Property Appraisals

When the building sells with the business, value them separately and on purpose.

Owner-occupied buildings, practices sold with their premises, shops with the lot they sit on. Real estate that changes hands alongside a business needs its own number, because the lender, the tax return and the purchase agreement each treat it differently from goodwill.

REAL ESTATE VALUATION AND OPINION OF VALUE

An opinion of value for the transaction, the plan and the negotiation.

Kerdai prepares real estate valuations and opinions of value for commercial property that is sold with or alongside a business, for transaction, planning and negotiation purposes. The work draws on the team's graduate training in international real estate, a licensed Florida real estate sales associate on the team, and the same source-document discipline we apply to earnings: leases, rent rolls, operating statements, tax assessments and comparable sales.

Kerdai is not a state-certified general real estate appraiser.

WHEN IT IS USED

Three situations we see most often.

  • Business and building sold together. A single asking price covers both. The buyer, the bank and the seller's CPA each need to know how much of it is real estate.
  • Lease versus own analysis for a buyer. Whether to buy the building with the business or lease it back from the seller, and what each path does to debt service and cash at closing.
  • Allocation of purchase price. The split between business and real estate for the lender's loan structure and for the purchase price allocation on the tax return, supported in writing.

SOP 50 10 8.1, IN BRIEF

What changed on October 1, 2026

  • On 7(a) change-of-ownership acquisitions with a business purchase price of $3 million or more, the lender must commission a Quality of Earnings report. Purchase price is measured before equity injection and seller notes.
  • A report commissioned by the buyer, the seller or the broker does not satisfy the requirement.
  • Every change of ownership requires a business valuation from a Qualified Source (ASA, CBA, ABV, CVA or BCA).
  • SBA guidance directs the preparer to reconcile reported earnings to IRS account transcripts. Kerdai obtains them under one authorization and shares them with the bank, so the borrower is not asked twice.
  • Lenders run debt-service coverage on each historical year. Our adjusted earnings are stated by year for that reason.
